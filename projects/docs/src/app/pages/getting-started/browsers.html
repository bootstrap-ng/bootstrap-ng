Browsers and devices

The browsers bootstrap-ng supports, on desktop and mobile.

Supported browsers

bootstrap-ng supports the browsers that both Bootstrap 6 and Angular support: the latest versions of the major browsers, down to these minimum versions.

Browser Desktop Mobile
Chrome 138 and later Latest on Android
Edge 138 and later —
Firefox 147 and later Latest on Android
Safari 18 and later on macOS 18 and later on iOS and iPadOS

Other browsers built on Chromium, WebKit or Gecko, such as Opera, Samsung Internet and the browsers in iOS apps, generally work when they are based on one of these versions. Internet Explorer and the old, non-Chromium Edge are not supported.

Platform features

bootstrap-ng uses the browser's own elements and APIs instead of polyfills or extra libraries, so it needs browsers that support them:

  • <dialog> for dialogs and drawers, with focus trapping and Escape from the browser.
  • <details> for accordions, so their content is found by the browser's find in page.
  • ResizeObserver and IntersectionObserver for components that follow their size or position, such as nav overflow, scrollspy and tables.
  • Intl for dates, numbers and plural forms in the locale you set.

Mobile devices

Components work with touch, mouse and keyboard, and follow Bootstrap's responsive breakpoints. For mobile layouts, a drawer can open from the bottom of the screen as a sheet. Test on real devices or simulators as well as in your desktop browser's device emulation, which doesn't reproduce on-screen keyboards or touch scrolling.
